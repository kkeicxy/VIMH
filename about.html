Who we are

About Veritas Institute

Veritas Institute Medical Hospital is an academic medical centre in Batangas dedicated to evidence-based care, medical education and service to the community.

Mission, vision and values

Mission

To deliver safe, compassionate clinical care while training the next generation of health professionals for Batangas and beyond.

Vision

To be the region’s most trusted academic hospital—known for clarity, clinical excellence and respect for every patient.

Values

Integrity, stewardship, collaboration and continuous learning guide our wards, clinics and classrooms.

Our story

A short history

  1. 1998

    Founding of the Veritas Institute teaching clinic to support medical education in Batangas.

  2. 2008

    Expansion into a full-service hospital with emergency, diagnostics and specialty outpatient clinics.

  3. 2018

    Opening of the academic tower and simulation centre for residents and nursing students.

  4. Today

    A multi-specialty campus serving patients with board-certified teams and 24/7 emergency care.
