Frequently asked questions

How do I book an appointment?

Use our online booking form, call patient services at (043) 000-0000, or ask at the outpatient desk. You can request a specific doctor or the next available clinician in a specialty.

What should I bring to my visit?

Bring a valid ID, any referral letters, a list of current medications and your PhilHealth or HMO card if applicable. Arrive 15 minutes early for registration.

Is the emergency department open overnight?

Yes. Emergency care is available 24 hours a day, every day. For life-threatening emergencies, call (043) 000-0000 or go directly to the ED entrance.

How do I access the patient portal?

Enrolment is offered at registration desks after your first visit. Once activated, you can view appointments, results and prescriptions online.

Do you accept PhilHealth and HMO cards?

Yes for many services, subject to coverage rules. Confirm benefits with your provider and our billing desk before elective procedures.

How can I apply for a career at VIMH?

Select “Careers” in the contact form topic, or email hr@vimh.example with your CV. Clinical and academic openings are posted periodically.
